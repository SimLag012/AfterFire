The final sequential release run opened the overview in 1.10 s on desktop and 0.65 s on mobile, then restored a persisted inner view in 0.26 s and 0.20 s respectively. Settled zoom/pan updates including Leaflet animation took 0.24–0.48 s. Cold here describes the browser only. Provider latency, the basemap and catalog availability vary. FIRMS caps and per-client limits remain enforced; bounded refinement can require several passes. Scientific cache deletion and production-worker crash injection were outside the verification procedure.
