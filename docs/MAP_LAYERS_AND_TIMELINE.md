# Map layers and time

The Explorer Layers panel toggles hotspots/visual clusters, candidate focus, place labels/borders, terrain and dated NASA imagery. Hotspot color may use confidence, instrument or categorical observation date; exact values remain in the popup. Opacity affects fire markers and scientific raster overlays. Mixed-date visual aggregates retain their own aggregate meaning, with date range and sensor counts; they are not scientific event candidates.

Selecting an event enables read-only lookup of published scientific products. Burned Area, NDVI and EVI can be displayed directly on the global canvas when a persisted result exists. The composite selector uses actual stored dates/assets only. NDVI/EVI use the existing −0.2 to 1 scale and transparent QA rejection. MCD64A1 geometry and estimated BurnDate remain distinct from FIRMS observation dates.

Recovery Story, vegetation anomalies, Recovery Intelligence, persistent zones, Reburn History and Recovery Gap stay in the existing event analysis and territory flows, linked from the panel. They are event-scoped scientific products rather than fabricated global overlays. Unacquired results are labelled unavailable; no acquisition/job is started by opening Layers, changing visibility or checking a result.

The bottom Previous/Next controls move the selected FIRMS interval by its inclusive duration. Scientific composite dates remain separate. All fire observation dates/times use UTC. A rolling 24-hour archive filter includes acquisition time, not just two calendar dates. No interpolated fire detections or invented scientific frames are generated.
